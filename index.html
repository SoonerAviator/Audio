How the grid works

Rows 1–2
16 steps of the selected track. Shift-click for an accent.
Row 3
Velocity for new steps.
Row 4
Song chain. Tap + to add the pattern you're editing, tap an entry to jump to it.
Row 5
Patterns A–H.
Row 6
Looper: record / play / overdub, stop, save slot (opens the slot picker), undo, clear (tap twice), bars, source, monitor. Recording starts the moment you press.
Pads
Play and select tracks.
Row 11
Sample tools for the selected track: load an audio file, record from the mic (tap again to stop), restore the built-in sound, and choke (tracks with choke on cut each other off, like open and closed hi-hats).
COPY
Arm, then tap a pattern to copy this one into it.
CLR
Arm, then tap a pattern, a chain entry or a track pad to clear it.
TAP
Tap in time to set the tempo.

Page through the song chain, 8 bars at a time.
